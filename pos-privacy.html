Privacy Policy

1805 POS — the register app for coffee shops · Effective November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information the 1805 POS register app ("the app") and the service behind it handle, why, and what you can do about it. The service is operated by 1805 Coffee Shop, Laur, Nueva Ecija, Philippines ("we", "us", "the platform").

Two kinds of people, two roles.

If you are a shop owner or staff member using the app, we decide how your account information is handled and this policy speaks to you directly. If you are a customer of a shop that uses the app, that shop decides what is collected about you and why — it is the personal information controller of its own customers' data — and we handle that data only on the shop's behalf and instructions, as its processor.

2. Information a shop handles about its customers

A shop that uses the app, and the companion customer app, may record the following about its own customers. The shop is the controller of this data. We store and process it for the shop, under the shop's instructions and our agreement with it, and do not use it for our own purposes.

  • Name, mobile number and email address, and a profile photo if the customer adds one.
  • Purchase history at that shop: orders, what was bought, how it was paid, pickup times and any notes the customer typed.
  • Loyalty points, rewards, referral and game activity at that shop.
  • Wallet (store credit) balance and its history of top-ups, spending and refunds at that shop, and gifts sent or received.
  • Push notification tokens for the customer's own devices, used to tell them about their orders.

Each shop's records are kept separate from every other shop's. A customer who uses more than one shop has a separate record, balance and history at each, and one shop cannot see another's.

If you are a customer and want to see, correct or delete what a shop holds about you, ask that shop — or use the account-deletion option in the customer app. We will help the shop respond, and will act on a request passed to us by the shop.

3. Why we process information, and on what basis

  • To provide the service to a shop — performing our agreement with the shop: accounts, the register, reports, online orders, payments.
  • To keep the service secure and working — our legitimate interests: preventing abuse, limiting repeated sign-in or invitation attempts, diagnosing faults.
  • To meet legal obligations that apply to us.
  • For a shop's customers — on the shop's behalf and for the purposes the shop has told its customers about.

5. How information is protected

  • Data travels over encrypted connections (TLS).
  • Each shop's data is separated in the database by rules enforced on the server, so one shop's sign-in cannot read another shop's records.
  • Passwords and staff PINs are stored only as hashes. Payment secrets are held where no client application can read them.
  • Sensitive actions — changing payment credentials, deleting the store account — require the owner's password to have been entered recently.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify the National Privacy Commission and the people affected as the law requires, and will notify an affected shop without undue delay so it can meet its own obligations.

6. How long information is kept

  • A shop's account and records are kept for as long as the store account exists.
  • A customer who deletes their account in the customer app has their sign-in removed and their record at each shop anonymised: name, phone, email and photo are cleared and loyalty points and wallet balance are forfeited. The orders themselves remain with the shop as business records of sales that took place, no longer linked to a person's contact details.
  • Notification tokens are removed when the device stops accepting them, when their lease expires, or when the account is deleted.
  • Backups made by our hosting provider may retain deleted data for up to 30 days before it is overwritten.

7. Deleting a store account

The owner can delete the store account in the app at Settings → Account → Delete store account. This permanently removes the shop from the service: its menu, staff roster, settings, sales and expense records, the records it holds about its customers, its connected payment credentials, and the owner's sign-in. It cannot be undone.

Deletion is refused while the shop's customers still hold wallet balances or unredeemed gifts there, or while a payment is still being processed — that money belongs to customers and must be settled first. Shops are responsible for keeping any records the law requires them to keep (for example for tax purposes) and should export what they need before deleting.

8. Your rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012

Under Republic Act No. 10173 and its implementing rules, you have the right to:

  • be informed that your personal data is being processed, and how;
  • access the personal data held about you;
  • object to processing, including for direct marketing;
  • rectify data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • erasure or blocking of data that is no longer necessary, was unlawfully obtained, or that you withdraw consent for;
  • data portability — to obtain a copy in a commonly used electronic format;
  • damages where you are harmed by inaccurate, unlawfully obtained or misused data; and
  • lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

How to exercise them

  • Shop owners and staff: much of your information can be seen and corrected in the app (store details, staff roster). For anything else, write to patrick@demirix.com. We will answer within a reasonable time and may need to confirm that you are the account holder first.
  • Customers of a shop: contact the shop, which controls your data, or use the customer app. If you write to us, we will pass your request to the shop concerned and help it respond.

9. Receipts are not official receipts

Receipts and reports produced by the app are records for the shop and its customer. They are not Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) official receipts or invoices, and the app is not a BIR-accredited sales system. Each shop is responsible for issuing official receipts or invoices in the way its registration requires.

10. Children

The app is a business tool for shop owners and their staff and is not directed to children.

11. Changes to this policy

We will post any change on this page and update the effective date. If a change materially affects how we handle personal data, we will tell store account holders by email or in the app before it takes effect.
