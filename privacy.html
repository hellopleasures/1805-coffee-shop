Privacy Policy

1805 Coffee Shop app · Effective July 15, 2026

This policy describes how the 1805 Coffee Shop iPhone app ("the app", "we") collects and uses your information. The app exists to let you order ahead, pay, and earn loyalty rewards at 1805 Coffee Shop.

What we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address, and mobile number, provided when you create an account.
  • Orders and purchases — the items you order, pickup times, amounts paid, and your order history.
  • Loyalty data — points earned and redeemed, and rewards claimed.
  • Push notification token — a device identifier used solely to send you order-status updates, if you allow notifications.

How we use it

  • To prepare and hand over your orders.
  • To credit loyalty points and let you redeem rewards.
  • To notify you when your order status changes.
  • To provide customer support and process refunds.

Payments

QR Ph payments are processed by PayMongo, a licensed Philippine payment processor. Your payment is completed inside your own e-wallet or banking app (such as GCash or Maya) — we never see or store your e-wallet credentials, card numbers, or banking details. We receive only confirmation that a payment succeeded, its amount, and a payment reference.

Where your data lives

Your data is stored securely with Supabase, our cloud database provider, protected by row-level security so your records are only accessible to you and the store.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not show ads.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. This removes your sign-in and personal details immediately. Loyalty points are forfeited; completed orders remain in the store's sales records as required for accounting.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
