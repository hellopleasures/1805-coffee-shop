Common questions

My payment went through but the order isn't showing. Pull down to refresh the Orders tab. If it still doesn't appear within a few minutes, email us with your order number and payment time — refunds are always available for unfulfilled orders.

How do loyalty points work? You earn points automatically on every purchase — in-app or at the counter with your member QR code. Collect enough and your next drink is free, redeemable right from your cart.

How do I delete my account? In the app: Profile → Delete account. Your sign-in and personal details are removed immediately.
