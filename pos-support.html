What 1805 POS is

1805 POS is a point-of-sale register for coffee shops in the Philippines, for iPhone and iPad. It takes orders at the counter, records cash, e-wallet and QR Ph payments, and keeps your menu, inventory, staff, expenses and sales reports in one place. It keeps selling when the connection drops and sends those sales when it returns.

If you choose to, your customers can also order ahead, load a wallet, collect loyalty rewards and send gifts at your shop through the companion customer app.

Prices and reports are in Philippine pesos. There is no VAT or tax line, and receipts from the app are not BIR official receipts or invoices — issue those the way your registration requires.

Getting an account

  1. Install 1805 POS from the App Store on an iPhone or iPad.
  2. On the first screen choose to create an account and enter your store's name, your name, your email address and a password.
  3. If you are asked to confirm your email, open the link we send you, then return to the app and sign in.
  4. Add your menu and your staff. Each staff member gets a PIN for signing in at the counter.

One store account is one shop. To accept QR Ph payments, connect your own PayMongo account in Settings; to appear in the customer app, switch on your listing in Settings (listings are reviewed before they appear).

Resetting your password

  1. On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password?
  2. Enter the email address your store account uses and tap Email me a reset link.
  3. Open the link in the email on any device and choose a new password, then go back to the app and sign in.

Nothing after a few minutes? Check the spam folder and the spelling of the address. A link is only sent to an address that has a store account, so no email means there is no account for it.

A staff member forgot their PIN

PINs are stored only in scrambled form, so nobody can look one up — not even us. An owner can set a new PIN for that person in the staff list.

Giving someone read-only access

An owner can invite an accountant, partner or investor to view sales, inventory and reports without being able to change anything. The person invited must sign in and accept the invitation before they can see anything, and the owner can revoke access at any time. A viewer can also leave a store on their own.

Deleting your store account

You can delete your store account, and the shop's data with it, from inside the app:

Settings → Account → Delete store account

  • You will be asked for your password again if you have not entered it in the last few minutes.
  • What is removed: the shop's menu, staff roster, settings, sales and expense records, the records it holds about its customers, its connected payment credentials, and your sign-in. It is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • When it is refused: while your customers still hold wallet balances or unredeemed gifts at your shop, or while a payment is still being processed. That money is your customers'; settle it first, then try again.
  • Before you delete: save any records you are required to keep, for example for tax purposes. Sales made offline must have finished sending.
  • If deletion stops part-way, nothing is left half-removed: sign in and run it again, or write to us.

Cannot get into the app? Write to patrick@demirix.com from the email address on the account and we will delete it for you after confirming it is yours.

Common questions

A QR Ph payment was made but the sale did not complete

Payments are confirmed by PayMongo and can arrive a little late. Check Incoming Orders and the day's payments before taking payment again. Money paid to your own PayMongo account appears in your PayMongo dashboard.

The register is offline

Cash and manually recorded e-wallet sales continue and are sent when the connection returns. QR Ph, wallet spending and loyalty redemption need a connection.

A customer wants their data corrected or deleted

Customers can delete their own account in the customer app. As the shop, you are responsible for answering your customers' privacy requests; see the Privacy Policy, and write to us if you need help.
