Delete Your Account

1805 Coffee Shop app

You can delete your 1805 Coffee Shop account and its associated data at any time. This applies to the app published by 1805 Coffee Shop under the package name com.alphaton.brewcustomer.

Delete from within the app

  1. Open the 1805 Coffee Shop app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you:

  • Email: iammpjdcp@gmail.com
  • Phone: +63 976 030 3240

We will action verified requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in credentials (email and password) and profile details — name, email address, and mobile number.
  • Your loyalty points balance and reward history (points are forfeited on deletion).
  • Your saved push-notification token.

What is retained

Records of completed and paid orders are kept in the store's sales and accounting records as required for tax and bookkeeping purposes. These records are dissociated from your deleted profile and are not used to contact or identify you afterward.
